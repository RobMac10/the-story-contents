# Book One timeline — Deep Time to Federation

Companion timeline for Book One of *Reopen the Record: Australian Histories*, from deep time to Federation and the vote in 1902.

Live at https://robmac10.github.io/the-story-contents/book-one/

Rebuilt from a writing draft so the page matches the other companion apps: noindex and a robots.txt Disallow at the repo root, a QR code, images that fit the screen and tap to enlarge, no audio, and no year levels or case numbers on student cards. Rock art is not shown. Photographs of deceased Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander people are not used.

The Blue Mountains stop is an outline only; a full companion case has not been published.

Rebuild with `python3 build_page.py` from this folder (source: `/workspace/muse/extracted/book-one-timeline.html`).
